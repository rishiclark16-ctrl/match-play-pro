# MATCH Golf — Brand Identity

## App Name & Tagline
- **Name:** MATCH
- **Full Name:** MATCH — Golf Scoring
- **Tagline:** Score. Bet. Settle Up.
- **Category:** Sports / Golf
- **Platform:** iOS (primary)

---

## Brand Personality
- **Bold** — high contrast, confident, no decoration for its own sake
- **Technical** — data-forward, monospace numbers, precise layout
- **Social** — built around groups, competition, money on the line
- **Outdoor-ready** — legible in sunlight, big touch targets, fast

The aesthetic is a modern sports tech product — closer to Robinhood or Linear than a traditional golf app. No plaid, no clichéd greens, no country club vibes.

---

## Voice & Tone
- Direct and short. No fluff.
- Competitive but fun — stakes are real but the app feels effortless
- Use golf vocabulary naturally (birdie, press, skins, Nassau) — assume the audience knows golf
- Avoid corporate-speak. Write like a golfer texting their group chat.

**Good:** "Your tab just settled. You're up $14."
**Bad:** "Congratulations! Your financial transaction has been processed."

---

## App Store Description (current)
MATCH turns your Saturday round into a real competition.

Track stroke play, skins, Nassau, match play, wolf, and best ball — all at once, in real time. Add money, handicaps, and auto-presses. Voice-score holes hands-free. Settle up the moment you walk off 18.

Built for groups who actually play for something.

**Key features:**
- Real-time multiplayer scoring with live leaderboard
- 6 simultaneous betting games with automatic settlement
- Voice scoring — just say the score, no tapping required
- House Game Builder — describe your group's rules in plain English, AI builds the format
- Auto-press detection, carryovers, handicap strokes per hole
- Offline support — scores sync when you're back in range
- Friend system, golf groups, running ledger

---

## Target Audience
- Golfers who play for money (even just $5 Nassau)
- Ages 25–55, male skew, plays 1–3x per month
- Plays in a regular group or with the same friends
- Frustrated by paper scorecards and Venmo math after the round
- Already uses apps like Grint or 18Birdies for handicaps — MATCH is for when money is involved

# MATCH Golf — Color System

## Primary Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Background | Warm Off-White | `#F5F5F0` | App background, all screens |
| Foreground | Near Black | `#0A0A0A` | Primary text, CTAs, headers |
| Card | Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, inputs, sheets |
| Border | Soft Gray | `#DCDCD5` | Card borders, dividers |
| Muted | Light Gray | `#EAEAE4` | Disabled states, subtle fills |
| Muted Text | Mid Gray | `#787878` | Secondary labels, metadata |

## Accent Colors

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| **Hero Accent** | Chartreuse Yellow | `#F0EE3A` | Winner badge, PRO badge, key highlights — use sparingly |
| **Live / Success** | Deep Green | `#1B7832` | Live rounds, birdies, eagles, positive states |
| **Active** | Bright Green | `#22C55E` | Progress bars, active dots, positive deltas |
| **Warning** | Amber | `#F59E0B` | Bogeys, caution states |
| **Danger** | Red | `#EF4444` | Double bogey+, errors, destructive actions |

## Dark Mode
The app supports dark mode. Backgrounds invert to `#0F0F0F`, text to `#F7F7F7`. All accent colors remain the same. Do not use dark mode as the primary in marketing — use light mode as default.

## Color Do's and Don'ts
- ✅ Use `#F0EE3A` (chartreuse) as a pop of energy — 1 element per composition max
- ✅ Use `#0A0A0A` on `#F0EE3A` for the PRO badge treatment
- ✅ High contrast pairings: near-black on off-white, white on near-black
- ✅ Logo always on near-black (`#0A0A0A`) background
- ❌ Don't use traditional golf greens — the old penguin/green aesthetic is retired
- ❌ Don't use gradients — the brand is flat and crisp
- ❌ Don't use the chartreuse as a background color at large scale
- ❌ Don't place the logo on white or light backgrounds

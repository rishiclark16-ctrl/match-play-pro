## Mission Statement

MATCH Golf exists to make every golf round more fun, more competitive, and more social. We bring the real betting games, side bets, and trash talk that golfers already love off the course into a beautifully designed mobile app that handles the math, tracks the money, and keeps everyone honest -- in real time.

---

## Vision

To be the #1 companion app for recreational golfers who play for money, pride, or both. Every foursome, every weekend game, every group trip -- MATCH is on every phone in the cart.

---

## Value Proposition

| For | Problem | MATCH Solves It By |
|-----|---------|-------------------|
| **Weekend golfers** | Keeping score on paper, losing track of bets | Real-time digital scorecard + automatic bet calculations |
| **Groups/foursomes** | Everyone argues about who owes what | Instant settlement breakdown at the end of each round |
| **Competitive friends** | No way to track head-to-head records or all-time stats | Season leaderboards, all-time records, public profiles |
| **New-to-betting golfers** | Don't know how Nassau or Skins work | App handles all rules automatically -- just enter scores |
| **Voice-first users** | Typing scores while driving a cart is annoying | Hands-free voice scoring with natural language |

## Target Audience

### Primary
- Recreational/amateur golfers aged 25-55
- Play 1-4 rounds per month
- Play friendly money games (Nassau, Skins, Match Play, Wolf)
- Golf in regular groups or foursomes
- iOS users (primary platform)

### Secondary
- Golf league organizers
- Golf trip coordinators
- Casual golfers who want a better scorecard app
- Golf groups who want persistent leaderboards and stats

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | What They Do | Where MATCH Wins |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| 18Birdies | GPS + scorecard | No real betting games, no voice scoring |
| TheGrint | Handicap tracking + scorecard | Limited betting, no real-time multiplayer |
| Golf GameBook | Scoring + tournaments | Complex UI, not focused on casual money games |
| SwingU | GPS + instruction | No betting features at all |
| Spreadsheets / Paper | Manual tracking | Obvious |

**MATCH's Moat:** Deep betting game logic (7+ game types, 12+ prop bets), voice scoring NLP, real-time multiplayer, and settlement tracking -- all in one native iOS app with a modern, premium UI.

## Revenue Model

### Freemium with In-App Subscription

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Stroke play, match play, up to 4 players, 4 friends, CTP & longest drive prop bets, basic stats |
| **MATCH Pro (Monthly)** | $3.99/mo | All game types, all prop bets, 8 players, unlimited friends, full stats, settlement tracking |
| **MATCH Pro (Annual)** | $24.99/yr | Same as monthly (48% savings) |

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a new round?

Tap the "+" button on the home screen, select a course, add players, and choose your game format. You can share the round code with others to join.

How do I add friends?

Go to the Friends tab, then share your unique 6-character friend code or scan a friend's QR code. You can also search by email or phone number.

How are handicap strokes calculated?

MATCH Golf uses the course handicap and slope rating you provide to calculate strokes per hole based on the hole's difficulty index. Strokes are distributed starting from the hardest holes.

Can I use voice commands to enter scores?

Yes! Tap the microphone button on the scorecard and say things like "Mike 5, Tim 4" or "birdie for Sarah". You can also say "next hole" to advance.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes! Scores are saved locally and will sync automatically when you regain internet connection.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone, find MATCH Golf, and tap Cancel Subscription.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Settings (gear icon), scroll down and tap "Delete Account". This will permanently remove your account and all associated data.

My scores aren't syncing between devices

Make sure you're signed in with the same account on both devices and have an internet connection. Try pulling down to refresh on the home screen.

How do settlements work?

MATCH Golf tracks who owes whom after matches, but we don't process any payments. Settlements are just for record-keeping - you handle the actual payment between friends however you prefer (Venmo, cash, etc.).

What game formats are supported?

MATCH supports Nassau, Skins, Match Play, Best Ball, and Stroke Play formats. You can enable multiple games in a single round.
